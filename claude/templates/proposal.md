# Title as a claim

## Summary
The proposal in three sentences: what, for whom, and the one number that justifies it. Three pages at most; section length follows importance.

## Problem
What hurts today, with numbers and a side-by-side against the current way.

## Proposal
The main section and the longest. What changes, how it works at a glance, what it connects to. Detail lives in the design doc.

## Alternatives
Each option, including doing nothing, and what makes it unacceptable.

## Costs and risks
Effort, running cost, the top reasons it could fail, unresolved questions. Known mess gets a few lines.

## Ask and decision needed
One sentence: the decision, from whom, by when.
